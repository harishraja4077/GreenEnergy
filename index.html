FAQs

Common Questions Answered

Quick answers about our technology, installation process, and financing options.

How long does installation take?

Smaller rooftop systems typically install within 1-2 days; larger commercial or utility projects take weeks to months depending on permitting, site works, and grid interconnection.

Do you offer financing or leasing?

Yes — we provide multiple financing models including PPA, lease, and purchase with available incentives to lower upfront costs.

What maintenance is required?

Solar and battery systems require minimal maintenance; we offer monitoring and service plans to keep systems optimized and warranty-compliant.

Can systems be scaled later?

Absolutely — systems are designed with modularity in mind so you can expand generation or storage as needs grow.
